# Advisory-use charter (draft)

**Draft for counsel and security-officer review. Not approved for operational use.**

CredenceAI-Gov is proposed as an evidence organization and decision-support tool for authorized government analysts. It preserves submitted materials, records analysis provenance, displays evidence and uncertainty, and routes specified cases to human reviewers.

The platform does not make a finding of fact, certify that content is genuine, identify a person in media, or replace an accredited forensic laboratory. A hash establishes continuity of bytes since intake only. Absence of provenance or detected manipulation is not proof of authenticity.

The platform must not publish, remove, suppress, notify, refer for enforcement, or otherwise take consequential action automatically. Authorized people retain decision authority and must record required rationale. Cases affecting named people require an approved notice/grievance path before operational use.

Before pilot: name the accountable department and data owner; approve purpose, prohibited uses, classification, identity roles, retention/legal-hold policy, hosting and egress, incident handling, signing-key custody, reviewer staffing, user notice, accessibility and support arrangements. **Counsel and security officer confirmation required.**
