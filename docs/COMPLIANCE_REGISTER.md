# Compliance and legal review register

All entries below are **needs counsel confirmation** and/or security-officer confirmation. This register is not legal advice and does not claim applicability or compliance.

| Topic | Question for owner/counsel | Status |
|---|---|---|
| Digital Personal Data Protection Act and rules | Applicability, roles, lawful purpose, notice, rights, processor obligations, breach handling, retention | Needs counsel confirmation |
| CERT-In directions | Applicability, log types/retention, reporting timelines, incident coordination and hosting responsibility | Needs counsel/security confirmation |
| GIGW 3.0 / WCAG 2.1 AA | Applicable procurement accessibility standard and conformance evidence | Needs procurement/security confirmation |
| Bharatiya Sakshya Adhiniyam 2023, section 63 | Required certificate format, signer authority, process and whether generated package is admissible/supporting evidence | Needs counsel confirmation; no legal sufficiency claim |
| IT Act and applicable rules | Applicability to platform and handling/hosting | Needs counsel confirmation |
| Synthetic/deepfake content rules | Current applicability, notice/grievance obligations and prohibited claims | Needs counsel confirmation |
| Records retention and legal hold | Approved schedules, authority, scope, audit metadata retention, deletion/key destruction | Needs records officer and counsel confirmation |
| Government hosting/security audit | Approved hosting region/network, security audit, accreditation, lead time, incident requirements | Needs security/procurement confirmation |
| Open-source licenses | AGPL obligations (including MinIO), model/data licenses, redistribution and modifications | Needs counsel/procurement confirmation |
| Evidence package signing | Department key ownership, certification, custody, revocation and evidentiary process | Needs security officer and counsel confirmation |
